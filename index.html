About the owner

A centre you can walk into and ask.

I am Laxman Gautam. I started R K Jan Seva Kendra two years ago because too many people around Banjara were travelling to town for a single form, losing a day of work, and still returning without being sure it was done correctly.

Now most of that work can happen right here in Banjara, Post Bhatpar, Gorakhpur. Bring your documents, tell me what you need, and I will explain every step in plain words before I submit anything.

If you are not sure what to bring, just send a WhatsApp message first. I would rather answer a question than see you make a wasted trip.

  • Forms and documents checked before submission to avoid rejection
  • Charges explained clearly before work starts
  • Simple explanation in your own language, no jargon
